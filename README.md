"PROJECT DESCRIPTION:

PROBLEM: Discomfort of using bicycle routes due to intense car traffic

GOAL: Increase the popularity of bicycle trips among people who are afraid of dangerous routes

IDEA: SafetyScore

Progressive web app service built on OpenStreetMap data, HERE layers and an extensive Brouter algorithm, as well as historical data on road accidents supported by AI

The dedicated Safety Score calculation algorithm takes into account:

– current traffic intensity

– permissible speed for route sections

– road surface

– quality route

– slope

– historical data on road accidents, including black spots involving cyclists from the last 3 years


SOLUTION:

– AI support in trip planning

– Intuitive, simple interface for planning based on the user’s prompt

– Ready-made prompt templates for user modification

– Voice search

Navigation and warning:

– Sending the planned route

– Direct export to the Google Maps navigation

– Export in GPX format

– Warning about dangerous points

BENEFITS:

Popularization of tourism cycling

– convincing users thanks to the possibility of planning safe cycling routes

– improving the health and physical condition of residents

– significant impact on ecology

Increasing cyclists’ safety

– planning safe routes on cycling paths and local roads

– information on black spots

– current traffic intensity

Simple cycling application

– intuitive search interface with AI

– optimal and safe cycling route suggestions

– navigation with danger messages

Promotion of the Małopolska province

– increased tourism

– easy access to integrated information about the region

- popularisation of cycling routes

– development of the local economy
